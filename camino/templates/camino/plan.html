Climb and descent come from the guide's height profiles, worked out the same way as on each stage page. A walking day over 30 km is marked as a long day.

    Where will you stop?

      Choose a whole guidebook stage

      Pick any stage from the guide, wherever it starts. If it does not start where the day before ended, that day is flagged so you can fix the order.

      Send it to the app

      This plan has booking references or notes in it. They are in the file, the QR code and the link, so keep those to yourself and the people you choose to send them to.

      Opening it in the app

      1. Get the file onto your phone: share it from here, or email or message it to yourself.
      2. In the app, open Settings, then Backup and restore, then Restore backup.
      3. Choose the file. The app shows what it contains before anything is added.
      4. Your plan appears in the app next to any plans you already have. Nothing is replaced.

      Opening the same file twice does not make a second copy.

        Where will you stay?

          Prices, opening dates and phone numbers come from the guide and can change. Check with the place before you rely on them.

          Share this plan as a QR code

          Point a phone camera at the code. It opens this planner with the plan ready to open there, and from there you can send the file to the app.

          The plan is inside the code and the link, so anyone who has them can see it: your stages, dates, places to stay and any booking references or travel notes you added. Nothing is stored on our server.
